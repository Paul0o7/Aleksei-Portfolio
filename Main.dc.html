Finite Element Analysis (FEA) static study performed on our lever arm. Fixtures were placed at the square axle hole and a torque was also applied at the same area (because of the motor’s movement at that point). Gravity was accounted for (seen with the big red arrow) and the load from the game elements were included as well. This analysis was performed to see minimum requirements needed for the lever arm. Our constraints were the torque from motors powering the arm and the load from the game elements. This study proves that our design was enough to withstand loads, however some drawbacks is that this design does not account for infill amount from the actual part. The main purpose of this study was to ensure that the design can survive forces at MAXIMUM infill (aka solid PETG). From here we were able to begin reducing the infill of the part because we knew that the design itself is sound.
